# Prime Survival
## World Design

## Overview

Prime Survival takes place on a large island that combines a variety of natural environments. The island is designed to encourage exploration, survival, and discovery.

The player begins on the coast with very limited resources. As they travel inland, they encounter increasingly difficult environments and richer resources.

---

# Design Goals

The world should feel:

- Large
- Natural
- Dangerous
- Beautiful
- Worth exploring

Every region should provide new challenges and rewards.

# Player Progression

The world is designed so that:

- The coast is the safest area.
- Forests introduce wildlife.
- Hills introduce mining.
- Mountains contain the rarest resources and greatest risks.

This creates a natural progression without forcing the player along a fixed path.
