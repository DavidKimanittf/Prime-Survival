# Prime Survival
## World Master Plan

## World Philosophy

The world of Prime Survival is not just a map—it is the main character.

Every mountain, river, forest, and coastline should feel like it belongs there naturally.

Players should always have something interesting to discover over the next hill.

---

# World Type

The game takes place on a large island.

The island is completely open from the beginning.

There are no invisible walls.

Progression is controlled by player skill, equipment, and environmental danger instead of locked areas.

---

# Size Goals

Initial playable area:
- Medium-sized handcrafted island.

Future expansions:
- Additional islands.
- Offshore structures.
- Ocean exploration.

---

# Environmental Diversity

The island should include:

- Sandy beaches
- Rocky coastlines
- Dense forests
- Open grasslands
- Rolling hills
- High mountains
- Freshwater rivers
- Lakes
- Waterfalls
- Cave entrances
- Swamps (future)
- Snow peaks (future)

---

# Exploration Philosophy

Every 2–5 minutes of exploration should reward the player with at least one of the following:

- A useful resource
- A beautiful viewpoint
- Wildlife
- A hidden path
- A cave entrance
- A landmark
- A point of interest

The player should rarely feel like they are walking through empty space.

---

# Navigation

The player should naturally navigate using:

- Mountains
- Rivers
- Coastlines
- Forest edges
- Large rock formations

No minimap will be required during early gameplay.

Players should learn the island by exploring it.
