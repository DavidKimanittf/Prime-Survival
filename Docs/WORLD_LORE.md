# Prime Survival
## World Lore

## Overview

Prime Survival takes place on **Astera Island**, a fictional island located far from major shipping routes. The island is known for its rich ecosystems, dramatic landscapes, and mysterious history.

For decades, Astera remained largely untouched by the outside world. Small research teams, explorers, and mining companies occasionally visited, but no permanent civilization was ever established.

Today, the island has been abandoned.

Nature has reclaimed everything that humans left behind.

---

# The Player

The player arrives on Astera Island after surviving a catastrophic transport accident.

They wake up alone on a remote beach with no equipment, no map, and no clear understanding of what happened.

Their first objective is simple:

**Survive.**

As the player explores, they uncover traces of those who came before them and slowly learn more about the island's past.

---

# The Island

Astera Island is divided into distinct natural regions, each with its own climate, resources, wildlife, and challenges.

Examples include:

- Southern beaches
- Central forests
- Western cliffs
- Northern mountains
- River valleys
- Hidden cave systems

Each region encourages different survival strategies.

---

# Human Presence

Evidence of previous visitors can be found throughout the island, including:

- Abandoned camps
- Research stations
- Broken vehicles
- Weather monitoring towers
- Small docks
- Mining sites

These locations provide resources, environmental storytelling, and future gameplay opportunities.

---

# Storytelling Philosophy

Prime Survival does not force a traditional story.

Instead, players discover the island's history through exploration.

Every location should tell part of the island's story through its environment.

Players who simply want to survive can do so.

Players who enjoy exploration can uncover the deeper history of Astera Island.

---

# Future Expansion

Future updates may introduce:

- Journals
- Audio recordings
- Hidden laboratories
- New islands
- NPC survivors
- Story missions

These additions will build upon the established lore without changing the core survival experience.
