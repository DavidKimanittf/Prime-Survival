# Prime Survival
## World Map Specification

## Overview

Prime Survival takes place on Astera Island, a large handcrafted open-world island designed for exploration and survival.

The island is designed around natural progression instead of invisible barriers.

# Spawn Area

The player begins on the southern beach.

This area is intentionally forgiving.

Resources include:

- Small rocks
- Driftwood
- Bushes
- Fresh water nearby
